e-NAM (Electronic National Agriculture Market) is an online trading platform launched by the Government of India to connect farmers, traders, and buyers across the country. It provides farmers with access to a wider market where they can sell their agricultural products and get information about market prices. It helps improve transparency in agricultural trading, reduces the need for middlemen, and allows farmers to find better opportunities to sell their produce at competitive prices. Overall, e-NAM aims to make agricultural marketing easier, more transparent, and more beneficial for farmers.
Farmers can use e-NAM to get better access to buyers and market information, including prices and trading opportunities.
